Join the Section

You can join the Section through APSA, or subscribe to one of its two email lists.

Membership

A regular Section membership costs $25. Student membership is free. Joining the Section requires an APSA membership.

Join through APSA

Only members may vote in Section elections or on motions at business meetings, or run for positions on the leadership council.

Email Lists

To subscribe to either list, send an email with “Please Add Me” in the subject line, including your full name, title, and institutional affiliation. To unsubscribe, send an email with “Please Remove Me” in the subject line.
