Section Awards

The Formal Theory Section presents awards at the APSA Annual Meeting. Nominations for the 2027 awards are due by 11:59 pm Pacific time on March 1, 2027. The nomination form is not open yet.

Get Email Updates

Best Article by a Non-Tenured Scholar

Committee (2025-2026): Hülya Eraslan; Justin Fox; Germán Gieczewski; Bahar Leventoglu

This award is given to the author(s) of the best paper published in the previous calendar year by a non-tenured scholar. To be eligible for this award, an article must have been published during the previous calendar year and the recipient must not have tenure on the date the article is first published on a journal’s website. An article may only be nominated once and it may be nominated for either the year in which it is first published on a journal’s website (e.g., as an “OnlineFirst” or “FirstView” article) or the year in which it appears in a print issue (but not both).

Most Recent Winner (2026)

Elaine Yao. 2026. “Brook No Compromise: How to Negotiate a United Front,” American Journal of Political Science 70(2): 643-657.

Journal of Theoretical Politics Ostrom Award

Committee (2025-2026): Natalia Garbiras-Díaz; Alex Hirsch; Federica Izzo; In Song Kim
