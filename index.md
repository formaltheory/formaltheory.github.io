# Formal Theory Section

Organized Section of the American Political Science Association
{: .sub}

The Section is a forum for scholars who use game theory, social choice theory, mechanism design, and related mathematical tools to study political phenomena. A full Section website is in development and will be published here.
{: .lead}

## In the meantime

- [Join the Section](https://apsanet.org/Sign-In) by logging into your MyAPSA account and selecting "Add a Section" under "Membership Information"
- [Subscribe to the Formal Theory email list](https://groups.google.com/a/lists.berkeley.edu/g/formaltheory) for Section business and announcements
- [APSA's Section page](https://apsanet.org/membership/organized-sections/section52/) lists current Section leadership
